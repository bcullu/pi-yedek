Privacy policy

pi-yedek is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • drive.file: to create and manage the backup files it uploads itself. It cannot see other files through this permission.
  • drive.readonly: to download the owner's own files from Google Drive to the owner's own storage. It does not change or delete anything through this permission.

How data is handled

  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's own device before upload. Google sees only encrypted data.
  • Access tokens are kept only on the owner's own devices.
  • No data is sent to, shared with, or sold to any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; files created by the tool can then be deleted from Google Drive.
